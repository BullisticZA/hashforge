# HashForge Roadmap

HashForge is built in playable steps. Every update adds a major system and keeps the balance tested:
a sensible start should *usually* make money in the first game-year, but never guaranteed.

## ✅ v0.1: Foundations (playable now)
- **2D management view** (floor plan, dashboards) with **3D hands-on views** for building, inspecting and swapping parts
- Procedural 3D hardware with spinning fans, bloom lighting and exploded GPU views
- 35+ GPUs and 19 ASIC miners with real model names and approximate hash rates per algorithm
- Vendors (new), used marketplace (graded, refreshing listings) and the **junkyard** (random lots of parts)
- **Quality grades:** Scrap, Used, Refurbished, New, Golden Sample. These affect hashrate, power draw and failure rate
- **GPU crafting** from core + memory + PCB + cooler; salvage cards into parts
- Open-air rigs with PSU load limits, ASIC racks with PDU limits, cooling and heat throttling
- Power bills in your currency (Rand by default), editable tariffs, live exchange rates
- **Load-shedding** stages 1–8, plus solar (sun, seasons, weather), inverters (trip when overloaded), batteries (wear with cycles), generators
- Wear, failures and repairs; market cycles, difficulty growth, halvings, heatwaves, shortages and crashes
- **Prestige (Hard Fork):** reset for Fork Shards, spend them on endless permanent upgrades; each era is harder
- An advisor that watches your operation and tells you what's wrong

## ✅ v0.1.1: Realism update
- Every card stands on a **PCIe riser** with USB 3.0 cables to the motherboard and real power routing from the PSU into 8/16-pin sockets
- **Risers** fail (cheap ones more often); **SATA-powered risers can start fires**
- **Boot drives** (USB flash, SATA SSD, M.2 NVMe) and a **mining OS**: free Linux, or Windows with a licence, overhead, an 8-GPU limit and overclock tools
- **Thermal pads and copper shims:** GDDR6X cards memory-throttle until re-padded
- **Dust** that eats cooling, intake filters, **hot/cold aisle containment**, **evaporative coolers** that struggle on humid days, ASICs that get less efficient when hot
- South Africa: optional **daytime surplus tariff** (cheap 06:00–18:00, pricier at night)

## ✅ v0.1.2: Hardware expansion
- Catalog grows from 97 to **242 items**: 91 GPUs, 62 ASIC miners, and many more PSUs, frames, racks, cooling units, inverters, batteries, solar arrays, generators, risers and boot drives
- New hardware types with their own 3D models: **passive datacenter cards**, **blower cards**, **headless mining cards**, **water-cooled hydro miners**, **home-heater miners**, **tiny open-source boards**, **evaporative towers**, a **20 ft container pod**
- New mods: **fresh thermal paste** and **replacement fans** that restore worn cards
- Shop **search and sort** (profit, payback, price, newest)

## ✅ v0.1.3: Flow update
- **Goals:** 15 guided goals from your first rig to solar and ASICs, then endless profit milestones (each doubles), all with cash rewards
- **Build from the Floor** without opening 3D: installed parts, matching inventory with Add / Remove, and Auto-fill
- **Auto-install** for new GPUs, PSUs and boot drives (switch it off in Inventory); Inventory has "Install in Rig N" buttons
- **Offline progress** with a "Welcome back" report
- Repeated log lines (daily outages) collapse into one line with a counter
- Advisor tells you when every rig is full and what to do about it
- **Balance:** load-shedding settles around Stage 1 in summer and Stage 3 in winter, with short Stage 6–8 spikes instead of sticking at Stage 8; slower wear and failures; difficulty climbs more gently and eases in slumps
- Sell inverters, batteries, solar and generators from the Power page

## ✅ v0.2: Servers & Hosting
- **Servers screen:** mini PCs, NAS appliances, towers and rack servers built from motherboards, CPUs, RAM, drives, PSUs and GPUs, with real compatibility checks
- **Clients screen:** hosting offers (websites, game servers, email, backups, CCTV, media, file servers, dev VMs, render nodes, ERP, cloud storage, AI inference, VPS) with SLAs, monthly pay, satisfaction and renewals
- **Reputation** unlocks bigger clients; downtime and data loss cost you
- **Drive protection (mirror / RAID)**, drive classes with different 24/7 failure rates, SAS backplanes, ECC requirements, Windows Server licences
- **UPS units** keep servers up through load-shedding
- Servers on the 2D floor (IT corner) and in the 3D tour, 3D models for every case, board, CPU, memory module, drive and UPS
- **Save slots**, save-file download and upload, and in-game confirm buttons instead of browser pop-ups
- Grouped menu: Run, Buy, Build, Grow
- **Difficulty follows price** with a lag instead of only climbing: earnings swing up and down and recover after slumps, with a slow ~4.5%/year squeeze. Old saves are re-based
- **UPS units also carry mining rigs** for part of an outage, after servers

## 🔜 v0.3: Builders & Workshop
- **Thermal work:** repaste, new thermal pads, copper shims and plates: lower temps, less wear, more hashrate
- **Workshop tools:** screwdriver kit, multimeter, thermal camera, reflow station, BIOS programmer. Better tools unlock better repairs
- Junkyard and used-market server parts: pull Xeons, ECC RAM and SAS drives from decommissioned servers
- One-off IT gigs: build a gaming PC for a client, repair a laptop, set up an office NAS
- CPU mining (RAM- and cache-hungry) and **storage mining** (proof of space: plot and farm on your drives)
- Many more junkyard part types and data-centre clearout lots

## 🔜 v0.4: Network & Security
- Switches, routers, cabling and uplinks; bandwidth and latency matter for pools
- **Firewalls** and security upgrades; hacking attempts, malware and ransomware events
- Remote monitoring and alerts

## 🔜 v0.5: Empire & IT Partner
- **Host for clients:** websites, storage, backups, design and IT services on monthly contracts, where uptime matters
- Buy and expand **homes, warehouses and data halls**; several sites at once
- **UPS systems**, three-phase power, transformers, grid contracts
- Staff: technicians who repair, guards, network admins

## 🔜 v0.6+: Endless
- Research tree, contracts and customer orders
- Overclocking and undervolting
- Achievements, seasonal events, rotating challenges
- More prestige layers and rarer hardware tiers
